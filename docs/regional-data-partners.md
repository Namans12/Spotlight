# Regional data provider assessment

Research date: 10 November 2026. Provider capabilities and terms can change; confirm them before signing or importing data. No accounts, paid plans, or partner requests were created.

| Candidate | Published capabilities and India evidence | Access and constraints | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [TMDB / JustWatch](https://developer.themoviedb.org/reference/tv-series-watch-providers) | Country-level current TV subscription, free, rent and buy listings. It does not itself establish the India premiere date or which season is available. | Existing TMDB key; JustWatch attribution is required. TMDB's [FAQ](https://developer.themoviedb.org/docs/faq) describes non-commercial licensing and commercial licensing requirements. | In use. The calendar can show the source-provided TMDB watch link when it matches the requested title and India. |
| [Watchmode](https://api.watchmode.com/docs) | Documents upcoming and recent streaming premieres, episode-level availability and country filtering. Published materials reviewed do not establish actual India title, provider or season coverage. | [Release dates are paid-only](https://api.watchmode.com/use-cases/titles-leaving); [pricing](https://api.watchmode.com/pricing) lists Startup at $349/month on the research date. Confirm India availability, contract, retention and attribution for the chosen plan. | Candidate for an evaluated pilot; no project credentials are configured. |
| [JustWatch partner API](https://partners.justwatch.com/) | Partner API and exports cover streaming availability; published material reviewed does not establish India season-level premiere-date coverage. | Partner account and agreement required; confirm permitted storage, attribution, date semantics and country coverage. | Candidate; no partner access is configured. |
| [MovieGlu](https://developer.movieglu.com/v2/api-index/setup/) | Documentation lists India as an accepted territory and describes [film showtimes](https://developer.movieglu.com/v2/api-index/filmshowtimes/). That is not proof of current city, cinema or title coverage. | [Evaluation key](https://developer.movieglu.com/v2/api-index/quick-start-guide/) is country-specific. Its showtimes documentation says not to cache that endpoint, and nearby search requires location. Showtimes after midnight can belong to the previous cinema business day. Confirm plan, rights, cost and coverage before use. | Best-documented India showtime pilot candidate; no key or agreement is configured. |
| [International Showtimes](https://www.internationalshowtimes.com/faqs) | Documents cinema and showtime APIs, international coverage and title-ID matching. India city/cinema coverage was not independently verified. | Partner/API-key access and terms require confirmation. | Candidate; no project credentials are configured. |

For a pilot, verify a representative set of Indian cities, regional-language titles, scheduled premiere dates and selected seasons against cinema or rights-holder announcements. Measure coverage, update latency and mismatches separately from existing global first-air dates. Do not infer that a movie release date is a cinema showtime or that a current TV listing proves a season's regional launch date. Request location only when needed and with user consent; do not log precise location by default.
