## Spoiler and publishing rules

Questions, answer choices and explanations must remain unrendered until the viewer selects a film, confirms they finished it, and starts the quiz. Changing the film, closing or restarting, or navigating to another title resets confirmation. No watched-history writes, analytics or external search queries are sent for these choices.

Food connections default to hidden before watching. Only individually reviewed cookbook associations can appear without watched confirmation. External sources may contain spoilers; warn before linking. Do not infer sequel facts from an earlier film or allow series confirmation to stand for a different film.

Food serving instructions, time estimates and dietary adaptations are Spotlight suggestions, not official recipes or guaranteed dietary claims. Keep ingredient checks visible. Share previews include only a user-selected pairing still eligible under the current filters.

To add a quiz, verify an exact catalogue identity, at least three distinct facts against reliable sources, one unambiguous correct answer per question, and the spoiler scope. Store source URLs and review date in the pack. Add a test for the pack’s validation and any new scope boundary. Unsupported facts remain unpublished; there is no unchecked web-generation fallback.
